Santosh Pathak, PhD

Associate Professor of Mathematics · University of Utah Asia Campus · Incheon, South Korea

Research Interests

Navier–Stokes equations, partial differential equations, Fourier and functional analysis; mathematics education, functions and graphs, APOS theory (Action–Process–Object–Schema), and function composition.

Appointments

  • 2025–present: Associate Professor, University of Utah Asia Campus.
  • 2020–2025: Assistant Professor, University of Utah Asia Campus.
  • 2019–2020: Lecturer, Tribhuvan University, Nepal.

Education

  • PhD, Applied Mathematics, University of New Mexico, 2019.
  • MS, Mathematical Sciences, Florida International University, 2014.
  • MA, Mathematics, Tribhuvan University, 2007.
  • BA, Mathematics and Economics, Tribhuvan University, 2004.
