Academic work

Research & teaching.

Two connected fields of interest: the mathematics itself, and how people come to understand mathematics.

01 · Mathematical analysis

Applied Mathematics

My applied-mathematics research concerns the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations, including maximum-norm estimates, bounded and nondecaying initial data, half-space domains, and analysis of the pressure term.

Navier–StokesPDEsFourier analysisPressure analysisMaximum-norm estimates
02 · Teaching and learning

Mathematics Education

I study students’ understanding of functions and graphs, especially their transitions between symbolic and graphical representations. I use APOS theory (Action–Process–Object–Schema) to investigate conceptual constructions, including linear functions and function composition.

APOS theoryFunctions & graphsGraphical reasoningMultiple representationsTextbook analysis

About me

Learning, teaching, and staying connected to my roots.

I am an Associate Professor of Mathematics at the University of Utah Asia Campus in Incheon, South Korea. I earned my PhD in Applied Mathematics from the University of New Mexico and my MS in Mathematical Sciences from Florida International University.

My academic work combines mathematical analysis with research on how students learn mathematics. Beyond the classroom, I write poetry and essays, and I hope to build a lasting collection about my village and the people of my community.
